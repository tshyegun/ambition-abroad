The mission

When I moved to Japan from Sydney at 21, I couldn't find anything about how to actually build a business abroad.

Everything was travel, lifestyle, or surface-level advice. Nothing about real people running real businesses. So I built what I wish existed.

Each episode follows a founder who left home and built something that actually works. No lifestyle content. No vague inspiration. Just real decisions, real mistakes, and real playbooks.

The newsletter is where it goes deeper. Full breakdowns, real numbers, and the lessons you can apply. This is the resource I needed at 21.
